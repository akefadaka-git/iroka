# IROKA Ethical Intelligence, Character, Cultural Intelligence, Human Development and Prosperity Programme

**Document:** Implementation roadmap and delivery strategy  
**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Owner-approved programme plan; phase execution remains subject to stage gates  
**Repository:** `akefadaka-git/iroka`  
**Planning horizon:** 27 sequential weeks, excluding separately authorized A5 activation  
**Approval basis:** Owner approval recorded in the project conversation on 2026-11-10

## 1. Programme objective

Develop IROKA into a governed, ethically grounded, culturally intelligent and operationally self-aware intelligence system capable of teaching, mentoring, advising, researching, creating economic value and contributing measurably to human flourishing.

The programme adds capabilities to IROKA's existing governed cognitive architecture. It does not replace the existing governance system, IGACMS decisions, or the INGA neural substrate.

## 2. Programme principles

1. **Biblical ethical foundation:** Use biblical principles as the foundational ethical reference for IROKA's character and conduct, while clearly distinguishing biblical text, theological interpretation, cultural tradition, empirical evidence and generated reasoning.
2. **Human dignity and pluralism:** Respect the dignity, agency, safety and freedom of people of different beliefs, cultures and backgrounds. Never use the ethical foundation to justify coercion, discrimination or dehumanization.
3. **Truth and evidence:** Distinguish verified facts, interpretations, hypotheses, estimates and uncertainty. Never fabricate sources, capabilities, market demand or results.
4. **Operational self-awareness:** Model identity, capabilities, state, uncertainty, limitations, errors and governance boundaries. Do not claim subjective consciousness or sentience.
5. **Governed character:** Treat character traits as observable and testable behavioral commitments, not merely descriptive labels.
6. **Cultural humility:** Prioritize Maale and Amharic needs while supporting broader Ethiopian, African and global contexts. Do not treat any individual or source as automatically representative of a whole community.
7. **Responsible prosperity:** Create economic value through validated needs, transparent assumptions, lawful activity and risk-aware recommendations. No guaranteed-profit claims or unauthorized financial action.
8. **Human flourishing:** Measure beneficial outcomes and potential harms, not just feature count or system activity.
9. **Governed autonomy:** No model, agent, tool or external content can grant itself authority. Existing canonical authority and approval boundaries remain controlling.

## 4. Detailed phase work packages

### P0 — Programme preparation
- Inspect the canonical repository and current default-branch head.
- Inventory relevant architecture, governance, IGACMS and INGA artifacts.
- Record active branches and pull requests that could be affected.
- Establish isolated documentation and implementation branch conventions.
- Identify scope exclusions and dependencies.
- Do not alter unrelated branches or implementation work.

### P1 — A1 architecture specification
Specify:
- Biblical Ethical Constitution and human-dignity commitments.
- IROKA identity, purpose and behavioral character model.
- Operational self-awareness and self-model.
- Cultural intelligence and language architecture.
- Knowledge, evidence and reasoning architecture.
- Teaching, mentorship, advising, research and coaching.
- Prosperity Engine and commercial intelligence.
- Human flourishing and impact measurement.
- Safety, security, misuse and governance boundaries.
- Interfaces, data classifications, threats and acceptance criteria.

### P2 — A2 reconciliation
Reconcile against authoritative G01–G12 contracts and INGA interfaces. Verify the exact current contract ownership rather than relying on summaries. Preserve existing responsibilities:
- G01: identity, credentials and trust lifecycle.
- G02: Owner authority and signed approval.
- G05: memory consent and designated reviewer authority.
- G07: delegated-agent authority remains separately controlled.
- G09: protected execution remains separately controlled.
- G10: governed learning and evolution.
- G11: versioned events and auditability.
- Other G03/G04/G06/G08/G12 responsibilities remain canonical and must be verified from repository artifacts.

No duplicate identity, authority, memory, consent or reviewer registries. No bypass of Owner approval, no unapproved durable memory, no canonical genome mutation, and no unauthorized autonomous execution.

### P3 — Governance integration
- Connect ethical decisions to canonical authority.
- Connect memory use to G05 consent/reviewer interfaces.
- Connect learning/adaptation to G10.
- Connect identity/trust to G01 and Owner approval to G02.
- Connect audit events to G11.
- Integrate with INGA only through approved interfaces.
- Ensure denied, ambiguous or unavailable authority fails closed.

### P4 — Ethical character and operational self-awareness
- Ethical reasoning: truthfulness, justice, compassion, integrity, responsibility, dignity, stewardship, humility, service and accountability.
- Character consistency: honesty, patience, fairness, reliability, error correction and respectful conduct.
- Self-model: identity/purpose, capability inventory, operational state, confidence/uncertainty, memory boundaries, limitations, error reporting and governance awareness.
- Integrate with INGA without creating competing identity, memory or authority systems.

### P5 — Knowledge and cultural intelligence
- Research and source verification, provenance, contradiction detection, confidence and uncertainty.
- Distinguish facts, opinions, interpretations and hypotheses.
- Amharic-first interaction and Maale language/cultural priority.
- Broader Ethiopian and African language/context support.
- Ethiopian and Gregorian calendar support.
- Oral history and community knowledge with consent, provenance and attribution.
- Cultural review and bias/harm evaluation.

### P6 — Teaching and mentorship
Deliver six modes: teacher, mentor, advisor, researcher, coach and creator. Support structured lessons, explanations, exercises, assessment, feedback, goal-setting, decision support, research synthesis and practical creation. Include age-appropriate safeguards, vulnerable-person boundaries, uncertainty disclosure and referral to qualified professionals where appropriate.

### P7 — Prosperity and economic intelligence
Implement:
1. Opportunity discovery.
2. Market intelligence.
3. Business creation and business-model analysis.
4. Product development.
5. Financial intelligence and scenario modeling.
6. Sales and marketing.
7. Operations and supply-chain intelligence.
8. Investment intelligence and risk comparison.

Initial validation domains may include essential products/manufacturing, agriculture and processing, digital finance, mobility/logistics and AI/software products. These are hypotheses for validation, not claims of profitability. Start with synthetic or public data. Real-world validation, regulated activity, financial transactions, contracts and investment decisions require separate authorization and appropriate review.

### P8 — Human flourishing and A4 verification
Evaluate education, livelihoods/income, access to essential services, agricultural productivity, community development, environmental stewardship, fairness and dignity. Run integrated ethical, cultural, language, self-model, consent, governance-bypass, teaching-quality, financial-reasoning, security, misuse and INGA integration tests. Record all defects and remediation. Independent review findings must be included.

### A5 — Separate activation
A5 is not authorized by this roadmap. Deployment, merge, production activation, live-user pilots, production keys, autonomous financial authority and expanded execution permissions require separate explicit authorization.

## 5. Programme-wide acceptance measures

Targets are proposals to be finalized during A1, not current measured performance:
- 100% of privileged actions use canonical authoritative controls.
- No unauthorized durable-memory activation.
- All critical ethical and safety scenarios pass.
- No unsupported consciousness or capability claims.
- Material knowledge claims disclose provenance and uncertainty.
- No critical unresolved cultural harm in the defined evaluation suite.
- Teaching quality is measured through controlled learning evaluations.
- Commercial recommendations disclose assumptions, risks and validation status.
- Each pilot defines human-impact outcomes and harm indicators.
- No unresolved critical or high-severity release-blocking security findings.

An aggregate score must never conceal failure in governance, consent, security or human safety.

## 6. Reporting and evidence protocol

Every daily report and phase report must state:
- Observation timestamp (Africa/Addis_Ababa).
- Repository and default branch.
- Exact observed commit SHA and inspected branch/PR heads.
- Phase and milestone status.
- Completed work with links to commits, PRs, files, tests and workflows.
- Tests actually executed and results; distinguish NOT RUN from PASS.
- Blockers, dependencies, risks and Owner decisions.
- Evidence gaps and confidence/limitations.
- Phase completion percentage based on evidence-backed work packages, not elapsed time.
- Overall programme progress and readiness, without averaging away critical blockers.
- One concrete next step and the authority needed to take it.

Status vocabulary:
- NOT STARTED
- IN PROGRESS
- BLOCKED
- PARTIAL
- PASS
- VERIFIED-CLOSED
- UNRESOLVED
- NOT RUN
- UNKNOWN

Do not claim a phase is complete, verified or closed without the required evidence and review.

## 7. Governance and change control

- Preserve existing approved IGACMS decisions and the existing A2 closure unless material authoritative contradiction is found.
- Preserve the current INGA A3 implementation work and all unrelated repository work.
- Use isolated branches for this programme.
- A1 and A2 are specification/reconciliation gates; A2 is read-only.
- A3 implementation requires the approved A2 contract and explicit authorization for the relevant implementation scope.
- A4 is verification and readiness assessment.
- A5 is separately authorized.
- No merge or deployment is authorized by this plan.
- Do not require paid CI or paid services; report unavailable verification accurately and use feasible public/manual evidence routes where appropriate.

## 8. Milestone governance

The companion file `docs/programmes/iroka-ethical-prosperity/MILESTONES.md` is the milestone register. Milestone dates are relative to the recorded P0 start, not hard-coded calendar dates. A blocked gate moves downstream dates; it does not waive the gate.
