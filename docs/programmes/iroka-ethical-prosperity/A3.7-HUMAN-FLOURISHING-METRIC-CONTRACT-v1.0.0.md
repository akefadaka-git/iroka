## 1. Purpose

Define candidate human-flourishing metric metadata and bounded synthetic-only impact comparisons. These are measurement contracts, not claims of achieved impact or permission to collect personal or community data.

## 2. Candidate domains

- Education
- Livelihood
- Health access
- Food security
- Water access
- Dignity and fairness
- Community wellbeing
- Environmental stewardship

This list is a candidate taxonomy. It does not settle OD-04, OD-05 or OD-06 and does not imply that every domain is in the initial pilot.

## 3. Metric definition contract

Every metric definition carries a stable identifier, domain, unit, direction, numerator label, optional denominator label, and adverse-outcome indicator. Rate metrics require a denominator and use integer basis points (0–10,000) to avoid floating-point ambiguity.

Directionality distinguishes higher-is-better, lower-is-better and monitor-only metrics. Adverse outcomes remain separately visible and must not be hidden by a positive aggregate movement elsewhere.

## 4. Synthetic-only observation and comparison

A3.7 observations are synthetic aggregate fixtures only. They cannot represent person-level records. Baseline and follow-up comparisons require matching metric identifiers and units and return descriptive change and interpretation.

The API always reports causal attribution as not established. A temporal association, before/after comparison or synthetic scenario does not prove that an IROKA programme caused an outcome.

## 5. Privacy boundary

A configurable minimum cohort-size policy suppresses small synthetic cells. This is a limited disclosure guard, not differential privacy, a legal anonymization determination, or approval to process real data. No participant identifiers, individual records, subgroup profiling, retention or real-world ingestion are implemented.

## 6. Governance gates

OD-06 remains OPEN. Candidate indicators only; real data collection is deferred. OD-04, OD-05, OD-07 and OD-08 also remain binding where relevant. Final metric selection, thresholds, target populations, pilot geography, data sources, consent, retention, high-impact review and intervention rules require their respective Owner decisions and evidence.

A3.7 does not authorize real data collection, training, memory activation, financial activity, protected execution, autonomous intervention, genome mutation, merge, release or deployment. A4 independent verification and A5 activation remain separate gates.
