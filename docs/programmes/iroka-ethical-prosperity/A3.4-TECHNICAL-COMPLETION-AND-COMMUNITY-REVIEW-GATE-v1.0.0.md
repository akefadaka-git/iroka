## 1. Purpose and correction

A3.4 builds IROKA's cultural-provenance and localization infrastructure. Maale-language expertise is not required to implement the platform's language-neutral architecture, localization data contracts, conservative script adapter boundary or calendar conversion utilities.

Qualified Maale language/community review is required only when the system makes Maale-specific claims or presents Maale spelling, grammar, translations, oral histories or cultural interpretations as validated or authoritative. This is a content-validation gate, not a prerequisite for IROKA platform engineering.

This record corrects the scope interpretation without changing or reopening the approved A2 architecture.

## 2. Technical scope

1. Retain source text and evidence provenance separately from any target-language candidate.
2. Validate source/target language tags and explicit translation lifecycle state.
3. Prevent untranslated records from carrying target text; require evidence references for review-evidence-recorded state.
4. Treat review evidence as descriptive only. It does not authenticate the reviewer, prove community authority or grant approval.
5. Provide a conservative script adapter: same declared script permits exact pass-through; unspecified or cross-script transformations return unsupported and preserve the original.
6. Provide deterministic, validated, bidirectional Ethiopian/Gregorian calendar conversion, including leap-year and month-boundary behavior.
7. Keep all transformations deterministic and side-effect free.

## 3. Maale content-review gate

The Owner reports that qualified Maale language reviewers/community authority have been identified. This is an Owner-provided assertion, not documentary evidence that any specific orthography, translation or cultural item has been reviewed or approved.

Before any Maale content is labelled authoritative, the relevant record must establish:
- the reviewer's verified identity and the canonical basis for their review authority;
- the exact content, version and language/orthography scope reviewed;
- the review outcome, date and any limitations or dissent;
- documentary evidence and provenance retained through canonical evidence/governance providers;
- any applicable consent, attribution and licensing conditions.

A3.4 must not create its own reviewer, identity, consent, delegation or approval registry. Reuse canonical G01/G02/G05/G11 providers when available. If a required provider or proof is absent, the affected content remains unverified and must not be represented as authoritative.

## 4. Explicit non-goals

- No invented Maale spelling, grammar, vocabulary, transliteration or translation.
- No assumption that one reviewer or source represents every Maale speaker or community.
- No ingestion of real community datasets, durable retention, model training or memory activation.
- No external network calls, model calls, protected execution or autonomous action.
- No change to A2 decisions, chromosome ownership or canonical governance providers.
- No merge, release or deployment authorization.

## 5. Verification requirements

The isolated branch must pass the repository's exact-commit Rust workflow, including formatting, compilation, all unit and contract tests, production-feature bypass checks and Clippy. The calendar tests must cover Ethiopian New Year, Pagume leap day, invalid dates, Gregorian century leap-year rules and round-trip boundary cases.

A3.4 technical completion is distinct from Maale content approval. CI success cannot establish linguistic correctness, community authority, independent assurance or production readiness.

## 6. Owner boundary

The Owner authorized completion of all technical work while preparing the community-review gate. This authorization does not authorize claiming authoritative Maale language approval without the evidence described above. A4 verification and A5 deployment/activation remain separate gates.
